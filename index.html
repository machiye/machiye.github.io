About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at City University of Hong Kong (expected graduation: August 2027). My research develops learning-based decision-making methods for complex industrial systems under uncertainty, combining reinforcement learning, stochastic modeling, and optimization. Recent work spans risk-averse multi-agent reinforcement learning for inventory management and lightweight domain-specific language models for supply chain disruption response.

Before CityU, I received my M.Eng. in Control Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology and my B.Eng. from Tianjin University of Science & Technology.
