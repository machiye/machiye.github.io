About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at City University of Hong Kong (expected graduation: August 2027). My research develops learning-based decision-making methods for complex industrial systems under uncertainty, combining reinforcement learning, stochastic modeling, and optimization. Recent work spans risk-averse multi-agent reinforcement learning for inventory management and language-model-assisted production and distribution re-planning under supply chain disruptions.

Before CityU, I received my M.Eng. in Control Engineering from Beijing Institute of Technology and my B.Eng. from Tianjin University of Science & Technology.

Research Projects

Risk-Averse Multi-Agent RL for Decision-Making under Uncertain Disruptions
Jan. 2022 – Dec. 2024 · Core Member · National Natural Science Foundation of China · PI: Prof. Wei Wang, City University of Hong Kong
Extended MAPPO with a sample-based GAE estimator optimizing a CVaR objective rather than expected return, keeping decentralized policies stable in the tail; evaluated on multi-echelon inventory control under stochastic disruptions.
Cooperative Multi-Agent RL for Autonomous Production Control
Nov. 2021 – Oct. 2024 · Participant · National Key R&D Program Youth Project · PI: Prof. Gang Wang, Beijing Institute of Technology
Formulated adaptive maintenance of degraded machines as a cooperative Dec-POMDP; trained decentralized policies with QMIX value factorization under CTDE.
Transient Performance Evaluation for Complex Production Systems
Jan. 2022 – Dec. 2024 · Core Member · National Natural Science Foundation of China · PI: Prof. Zhiyang Jia, Beijing Institute of Technology
Derived exact transient dynamics for two-machine one-buffer lines and a recursive algorithm extending them to general serial lines — the Markov-chain models behind the simulation environments used in the RL work above.
